Legionnaires' Disease

Legionnaires' disease is a an untypical pneumonia caused by bacteria of the genus Legionella, most often Legionella pneumophila, that can be severe and frequently fatal. It is a well-known and growing global health issue. Early detection, precise diagnosis and timely treatment are essential, and a multidisciplinary approach to healthcare is the most effective way to provide the best possible patient care.
